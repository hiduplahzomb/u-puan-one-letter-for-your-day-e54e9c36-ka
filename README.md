Entry for make.fun's "One Letter for Your Day" bounty: exactly one letter,
standing for the word that describes the sender's day.

**W — "whirlwind".** A day spinning out of control: everything arriving at
once, nothing standing still. The swooping shape of the letter matches the
word — the sound and the shape both whirl.

`main.py` writes the single character `W` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
and verifies it byte-for-byte.
